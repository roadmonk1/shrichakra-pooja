# CLIENT INFORMATION CHECKLIST — SRI CHAKRA DIGITAL EXPERIENCE

> **Document Purpose:** This checklist serves as the discovery questionnaire to be reviewed with the client/organizing family before replacing the demo prototype placeholders with verified, authentic information.
> 
> **Important Note:** In accordance with respectful spiritual guidelines, do not assert or publish unverified lineages, dates, or ritual claims without explicit sign-off from the family/patron.

---

## 1. ANNUAL POOJA + INVITATION REQUIREMENTS (V2 HIGHLIGHT)

### Event Details
- [ ] **Official Event Name:** Exact title for current and future annual poojas (e.g. *Annual Sri Chakra Mahapooja 2026*).
- [ ] **Confirmed Date:** Gregorian date & traditional Panchanga Tithi.
- [ ] **Start & End Time:** Exact time window for morning invocation, main pooja, and concluding aarati.
- [ ] **Location / Sanctum Venue:** Exact address, hall/mandapa name, and city.
- [ ] **Annual / Recurring Schedule:** Fixed progression of rituals (Suprabhata, Punyaha, Navavarana Archana, Annadana, Deeparadhana).
- [ ] **Event Description & Sankalpa:** Sacred message from the patron/priest to attending devotees.
- [ ] **Important Instructions:** Dress code, prasada timings, elder accessibility, and registration requirements.

### Digital & Printed Invitation
- [ ] **Existing Design Artwork:** Do you already have a designed invitation artwork (front & back)?
- [ ] **Invitation Image Format:** Provide high-resolution vector/image file (JPG, PNG, or TIFF).
- [ ] **Invitation PDF:** Printable official PDF file for devotees to download.
- [ ] **Preferred Invitation Wording:**
  - [ ] Approved English version
  - [ ] Approved Kannada (ಕನ್ನಡ) version
  - [ ] Approved Sanskrit (संस्कृतम्) version
- [ ] **Public Download Permission:** Should visitors be able to download the invitation directly from the website?
- [ ] **WhatsApp Sharing:** Confirm pre-formatted WhatsApp share message with dynamic date, venue, and web link.
- [ ] **Add to Calendar:** Confirm calendar integration (Google Calendar link and .ICS calendar file).

### Homepage Announcement & Countdown
- [ ] **Announcement Lead Time:** How many weeks/months prior to the event should the upcoming announcement appear?
- [ ] **Featured Status:** Should the upcoming pooja automatically be featured near the top of the homepage?
- [ ] **Countdown Display:** Enable live countdown timer (Days, Hours, Minutes, Seconds) until the pooja commences?
- [ ] **Post-Event Display:** What should happen after the celebration date passes? (e.g., display *"Celebration Concluded"* and shift to archive).

### Event Gallery & Archival Continuity
- [ ] **Which Previous Years Should Be Displayed?** (e.g. 2026, 2025, 2024, 2023, Milestone years).
- [ ] **Devotee Upload Permissions:** Who can submit photos? (Open to all attendees or volunteer team only?)
- [ ] **Photo Moderation Cadence:** How quickly should pending photos be approved via Admin Portal?
- [ ] **Public Display Policy:** Should devotee names and personal memory captions be shown publicly?
